🚗 I trained an AI agent to find the perfect moment to change gear — and it reduced fuel consumption vs a fixed-threshold strategy.

POST:
Gear shift timing has a measurable impact on fuel economy. Too early → lugging. Too late → overspeeding. The optimal point depends on speed, load, road gradient, and dozens of other factors.

In this project I built a custom transmission environment (Gym-style) and trained a Q-Learning RL agent to learn optimal shift points for a 6-speed petrol gearbox, rewarding low BSFC (Brake-Specific Fuel Consumption).

The agent was compared against a classic fixed-RPM threshold strategy:
⬆️ Smarter upshift decisions in transient acceleration
⬆️ Proactive downshifts before heavy load events
⬆️ Measurable BSFC reduction over a simulated drive cycle

This is the academic precursor to the PPO-based shift controllers being deployed in modern DCTs and AMTs by ZF, Schaeffler, and Stellantis.
